Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

We may use your email address to send important service notifications, such as changes to these policies.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

We do not integrate with advertising networks or analytics platforms that track you across other websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing any concerns promptly and transparently.
